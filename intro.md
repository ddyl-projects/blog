# Welcome to the Technical Blog
I’ve been using generative AI for quite some time now. For the most part, it serves as an elaborate search engine and a personal knowledge base. To me, it feels like having a brilliant colleague sitting next to me—someone I can bounce any question off of. (And yes, that includes asking them to rewrite my emails when I get a bit too verbose).
As long as I ignore the occasional "hallucination," the partnership works great. If you’re new to the AI space, a hallucination is just a fancy term for when the AI confidently makes stuff up because it fits a statistical pattern in its model.
While I mostly leverage AI for software engineering, I also use it for cooking recipes, DIY projects, and the occasional unsolicited financial advice. My wife, however, was less than thrilled to learn that a recent, "not-so-authentic" Pad Thai was actually a co-production with a chatbot. I had to do it, though... for science!
## Moving Beyond the "Vibe Check"
When it comes to specific tools, Gemini is my go-to for daily prompts, though I also regularly rotate through Copilot, GPT models, and GitLab Duo.
GitLab Duo actually represents a shift in how I usually work with AI. Apart from asking AI questions I’ve also been "vibe coding"—giving an AI with very little context a loose prompt and asking it to spit out short snippets of code. 
Recently I’ve decided it’s finally time to fully jump on the “Gen AI bandwagon” and explore some more sophisticated use cases.
## What This Blog Is (And Isn't)
This blog is going to be a chronicle of that journey. It’s not going to be another dry GenAI tutorial—there are already a million of those on YouTube. Instead, it’s a transparent look at my hands-on experiences, triumphs, and inevitable roadblocks. I'll also be sharing and linking the exact materials I'm using for my own self-study.
If you’re wondering what my very first pet project will be, you’re actually looking at it. I decided to use generative AI to build a simple blog website displaying these very articles.
I’ve documented the entire building process later on in the first post, so check it out to see if the AI actually delivered, or if it gave me the coding equivalent of that Pad Thai.

**A Quick Disclaimer**: _While all the thoughts, experiences, and technical ideas on this blog are 100% mine, I use AI as an editorial co-pilot to refine, format, and simplify the text. My goal here is to maximize my time experimenting with new tech and writing about it, delegating the final syntax polish to AI speeds up that pipeline._
